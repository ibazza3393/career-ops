## Case Study 1: Voyager — Kotahi Logistics Customer Portal
**Employer:** Kotahi Logistics (Subsidiary of Fonterra & Silver Fern Farms)
**Period:** January 2024 – Current
**Role:** Senior UX/UI Designer (embedded in project team with PM, Senior BA, FE Dev, BE Dev, end users)
**Portfolio URL:** https://ibarry.framer.website/

### Context
Part of a wider business transformation to re-energise Kotahi and future-proof its tools, systems and processes for AI readiness. Kotahi holds ~30% of New Zealand's containerised export volume and serves 50+ NZ primary exporters.

### The Problem
Multiple siloed legacy systems (Export Portal for NZ→World, Coastal Portal for NZ→NZ) made booking and tracking processes highly manual for Booking Specialists and customers alike.

### Approach
- Adopted **Double Diamond** framework (Discover → Define → Design → Deliver) within an agile working model
- **Discovery:** Qualitative research — interviews and focus group sessions with users; requirements documented in Confluence
- **Artefacts produced:** Customer Journey Lifecycle Blueprint, User Research synthesis, Customer Segmentation Model, Pain Point mapping, Strategy Partner Portal IA
- **Define:** User personas, Business Process Taxonomy, Low-fidelity wireframes
- **Design:** High-fidelity Figma prototypes for Customer Portal and Internal Portal; iterative design-feedback-iterate cycles
- **Deliver:** Dev handover, Jira board for program tracking

### Design System
- Co-built **Kotahi's first internal Design System** (Salesforce-based, aligned to Kotahi brand guidelines)
- Followed Salesforce design best practices; WCAG AA/AAA contrast compliance throughout
- Tool: Figma

### Impact & Scale
- Consolidated multiple legacy systems into a single unified platform automating planning, booking, tracking, reporting and invoicing
- Critical enabler of Kotahi's strategy — supporting NZ exporters to succeed globally
- Impacts 50+ NZ primary exporters managing 30% of NZ's containerised export volume
- Future-proofed and AI-ready infrastructure underpinning Kotahi's Go-to-Market strategy

### Key Proof Points (use in applications)
- "Led end-to-end UX for a business-critical enterprise portal consolidating multiple legacy systems — serving 50+ exporters and 30% of NZ's containerised export volume"
- "Co-created Kotahi's first internal Design System from scratch, built on Salesforce components and compliant with WCAG AA/AAA"
- "Applied Double Diamond and agile methodology across a full discovery-to-delivery cycle including qualitative research, journey mapping, persona development, IA, wireframing, and high-fidelity prototyping"
- "Designed for AI-readiness as part of a wider organisational transformation programme"

### Transferable Themes
- Enterprise portal consolidation (legacy → unified platform)
- Design systems at scale
- Stakeholder-heavy environments (PM, BA, dev, end users)
- Accessibility-first design
- Agile + Double Diamond methodology
- Supply chain / logistics domain knowledge
